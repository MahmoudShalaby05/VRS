Common questions answered

How do I create an account?

Start by clicking Login / Register, then provide your full name, email, Egyptian phone number starting with 01, and a strong password that meets our security requirements.

What documents do I need to rent a car?

You need a valid driving license and national ID. Make sure the information on your account matches your documents before pickup.
